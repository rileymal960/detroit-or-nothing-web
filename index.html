Cohort Curriculum

Paid Workshops for Young Men (Ages 16–24)

Structured for institutional sponsorship (BMO, foundations, workforce boards), our modules blend applied software creation with practical neighborhood leadership.

Tech Track 01

Applied AI & Software Prototyping

Using Detroit ON as a live sandbox, youth learn how to move from a napkin sketch to a functioning web app using prompt engineering, component structuring, and database basics.

Deliverable: Working Prototype
Economics Track 02

Civic Velocity & Local Economics

Teaching young leaders how the circulation of dollars inside Detroit zip codes impacts generational wealth. Covers grassroots organizing, merchant partnerships, and collective action.

Deliverable: Corridor Impact Strategy
Readiness Track 03

Workforce Agency & Digital Ownership

Translating real-world life experience into marketable professional value. Covers modern project management tools, high-agency communication, and digital portfolio building.

Deliverable: Live Digital Portfolio

The Architect Behind Detroit Or Nothing

Malik Riley Civic Program Manager, Software Architect & Detroit Native

Born and raised in Detroit, Malik has spent the last decade building programs at the intersection of youth empowerment, community resource mobilization, and technology. He is a proud graduate of Cass Technical High School and pursued his higher education at Michigan State University.

In addition to architecting and developing the full-stack systems behind Detroit ON, Malik operates Greenhub Detroit (formerly Planet Earth's Greenhouse)—a legacy, family-owned urban agriculture enterprise on Fenkell Avenue in Detroit. His work merges generational small-business heritage with cutting-edge artificial intelligence workflows.
