Case Study & Proof of Concept

Detroit ON (DON) Ecosystem

Originally launched in 2016 with over 300 Detroit merchants onboarded, Detroit ON creates a closed-loop civic economy: youth and residents take leadership in neighborhood causes and earn rewards at independent local businesses.

We recently rebuilt the entire system from the ground up using generative AI workflows—serving as a live, functional blueprint for our youth digital curriculum.
